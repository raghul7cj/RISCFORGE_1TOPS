02 / Arbitration

Decision and RR bins

GroupScenario binsWhat is sampled
Eligibility20813 individual sources x 4 thresholds x 2 source-enable states x 2 global-MIE states.

Fixed pair28Every unordered pair of the eight timer causes; the configured higher-priority source wins.

Example: UART pending, source enable on, MIE on and threshold 1 is one eligibility combination. UART belongs to class 1, so its rank is 2 and it passes threshold 1. The same source is blocked at threshold 2.

In addition to these bins, the test compares 196,608 arbitration snapshots: all pending vectors across four thresholds and six combined RR states, with all source enables and MIE asserted.
