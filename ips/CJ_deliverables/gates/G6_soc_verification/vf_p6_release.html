P6 is the last phase. It stamps the release gate G5 once on the frozen candidate manifest, runs the release partition, and issues the sign-off statement under three distinct signatories. This page states the conditions, the decision flow and what the engineering-baseline handoff of the stage 8 brief is not.

1. Objective and release decision flow

Sign-off is the conjunction of the release-level gate set, evaluated once per release on the frozen candidate manifest. There is no partial sign-off: a design with six of seven gates satisfied has six satisfied and one unknown. G5 requires G4 and G6 on the frozen manifest, the compliance report at the candidate identity, and the closed formal register (decision D-023). Figure 1 shows how P6 reaches or refuses a verdict.

9. Execution proposal

Proposal, not Rev B text. The stage 8 brief is an engineering-baseline handoff and is deliberately smaller than Rev B P6. It is not tapeout authorisation and not a G5 declaration.

  1. Freeze and identify. Record RTL and filelist hashes, tool versions, firmware images, commands and the Stage 6 and 7 manifests. Label each result core-only, SoC or selected APB device.
  2. Clean replay. Re-run the core applicable-test inventory and the SoC regression on that identity. Report PASS, FAIL, NOT RUN, NOT APPLICABLE and BLOCKED separately; never copy green results across RTL changes.
  3. Summarise evidence. Demonstrated functionality, checker-negative cases, executed coverage, stress stability, independent oracles; state which Rev B obligations (six-device models, ISS lock-step, formal, full coverage) remain open.
  4. Defect and risk ledger. For each open defect: observed, expected, reproducer, suspected area, impact, owner or next experiment; separate design bugs from unimplemented or unintegrated features.
  5. Handoff. Write verification_baseline.md and next_actions.md with the next three engineering actions (highest-risk RTL failures first, missing independent checker second, training-value experiment third).
  6. Full release, later. Only when P5 exit is met and the entry join passes, stamp G5 once and issue the Table 20.2 statement under three distinct signatories.

Not claimed. No tapeout readiness, no zero-defect statement, no invented coverage, no automatic pass for waived tests.
