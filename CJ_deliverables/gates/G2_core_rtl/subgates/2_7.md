## Testbench, evidence and remaining work

The legacy layered LSU testbench source is under `OLD files rtl-dv/verif/tb_lsu/`; the duplicate `OLD files rtl-dv/LSU verification/SV layered tb/` has identical SHA-256 hashes for its 12 SystemVerilog files. It includes interface, package, transaction, driver, monitor, reference model, scoreboard, sequencer, environment, coverage, assertions and top, plus a run script and implementation plan. The separate copied XSim log under `OLD files rtl-dv/lsu verification export/` reports 15 passes and 6 failures (21 transactions), warns that `dmem.hex` is missing, and refers to a different historical working path; it is not current-candidate evidence. The repository-root core-port package at `artifacts/core_port_20261109/` retains 13/13 passing candidate cases and 2/13 base cases, including word load/store with MDU overlap at waits 0/1/4. Its receipt binds the tested tree to merge commit `a91edb7e666d5c374af70809703f52eb5513d96f` and tree `3737bcf29b52efa4522310f066d684ec274e83d3`. This is scoped word traffic, not the full byte/half/alignment/error/effect matrix.
