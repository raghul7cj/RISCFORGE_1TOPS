3. Sampling, memories and missing checks

CSR/trap skeleton

Core top observes WB exception/commit/gated effects pre-edge and CSR state before the following falling edge. Separate phase-tagged records enter scheduler and scoreboard queues. Reset qualification means the default lifecycle supplies no architectural-event witness.

IMEM is an asynchronous placeholder NOP; DMEM is zero/no-stall/no-error tie-off, without storage or effects. The scoreboard consumes without checking; the assertion shell and coverage hooks are empty. Queue shutdown and zero failures do not prove correctness.

Hazard leaf

Unit guide: falling-edge drive → settled pre-rising-edge sample → immutable ID-tagged observation → user check → coverage callback. User sequence/checker/coverage remain unconfigured.

Source-defined guards reject unchecked/unrecorded samples and zero work. Plumbing uses two benign reset/release bundles and bypasses hooks. SKELETON_READY is infrastructure status; it was not observed in a retained runtime.

Pipeline + LSU

Pipeline guide: actual core/MDU probes, #1step pre-edge and +1 ps post-NBA snapshots. Explicit-image synchronous 4096-word IMEM; 32 KiB DMEM with bounded waits, byte effects and no store effect on error.

No token/effect oracle or bins. A high request after modeled completion can become a new local request ID; duplication needs architectural association. valid_W & ~exc_valid_W can stay high during a hold. LSU sizes/offsets/extension/alignment/error and real bus effects still need qualification.
